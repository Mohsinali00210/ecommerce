How it works
  • Within city: the customer's city is the city you ship from.
  • Zone A: same province as you. Zone B: any other province, or an unknown city.
  • The province comes from the address's state/province field, or from the city name.
  • Set product weights (kg) on each product. A product marked free shipping never adds weight.
