How payouts work
  • The customer sees the charge and the amount they will receive before submitting. The full amount is held from the wallet; the charge is kept, the rest is sent.
  • Manual: send the money yourself (bank app, JazzCash / Easypaisa business portal) to the account shown, then approve with the transaction reference.
  • Automatic: approving sends the money through the selected provider. A failure is logged and the request stays open to retry or reject (reject refunds the wallet).
  • Test payout (sandbox) lets you try the whole flow without real money: account numbers ending in 0000 simulate a failure.
  • Generic payout API posts signed JSON to your payout service or bank middleware (contract in UserPanel/payouts.py). JazzCash and Easypaisa disbursement APIs need a business agreement with them; connect them through this.
